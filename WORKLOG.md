(2) WHAT 'STRUCTURAL DENSITY MUST SCALE WITH THE BAND' ACTUALLY MEANS, stated as a criterion a compiler can be held to. The requirement is not satisfied by adding more tokens of any kind. To claim a high band a rule must demand genuine SYNTACTIC complexity: subordination, inversion, or strict dependency depth. Specifically it must increase mandatory-token count AND the number of DEP- or POS-constrained slots as the band rises, while keeping IN lists short and exact.
